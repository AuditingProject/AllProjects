### How these repos were matched

- All nine repos were reached from the `github.com/maple-labs` org that program-data.json links (via `maple-labs/maple-v2-audits`), enumerated with `gh api orgs/maple-labs/repos`. Only repos whose contract names match in-scope asset descriptions were cloned; `fixed-term-loan` / `fixed-term-loan-manager` were deliberately skipped because no fixed-term asset is in scope.
- `open-term-loan` — `contracts/MapleLoanFactory.sol`, `MapleLoan.sol`, `MapleLoanInitializer.sol`, `MapleRefinancer.sol` match assets 6, 7, 8, 13, 27 (MapleLoanFactory / Implementation / Initializer / MapleRefinancer, Open-Term).
- `open-term-loan-manager` — `contracts/LoanManagerFactory.sol`, `LoanManager.sol`, `LoanManagerInitializer.sol` match assets 1, 2, 3, 26 (LoanManager* Open-Term).
- `globals-v2` — `contracts/MapleGlobals.sol` and `contracts/GovernorTimelock.sol` match assets 4, 5 (MapleGlobals Implementation/Proxy) and asset 43 (GovernorTimelock).
- `pool-v2` — `contracts/MaplePoolDeployer.sol`, `MaplePoolManager.sol`, `MaplePool.sol`, `proxy/MaplePoolManagerFactory.sol`, `proxy/MaplePoolManagerInitializer.sol` match assets 9-12, 22, 23 (PoolDeployer, PoolManagerFactory/Implementation/Initializer, Pool, PoolManager).
- `withdrawal-manager-cyclical` — `contracts/MapleWithdrawalManagerFactory.sol`, `MapleWithdrawalManager.sol`, `MapleWithdrawalManagerInitializer.sol` match assets 16-18 (WithdrawalManager* Cyclical).
- `withdrawal-manager-queue` — `contracts/MapleWithdrawalManager.sol`, `proxy/MapleWithdrawalManagerFactory.sol`, `proxy/MapleWithdrawalManagerInitializer.sol` match assets 19-21, 24, 41, 42 (WithdrawalManager* Queue).

- `maple-strategies` — `contracts/MapleAaveStrategy.sol`, `MapleSkyStrategy.sol`, `MapleBasicStrategy.sol` match assets 29-37, 39, 40 (Aave / Sky / Basic strategy factories, implementations, initializers and instances).

## Rewards / payout terms

Rewards are distributed according to the impact of the vulnerability based on the [Immunefi Vulnerability Severity Classification System V2.2](https://immunefi.com/immunefi-vulnerability-severity-classification-system-v2-2). This is a simplified 5-level scale, with separate scales for websites/apps and smart contracts/blockchains, encompassing everything from consequence of exploitation to privilege required to likelihood of a successful exploit.

All bug reports must come with a PoC with an end-effect impacting an asset-in-scope in order to be considered for a reward.

Rewards for critical smart contract vulnerabilities are further capped at 10% of economic damage, with the main consideration being the funds affected in addition to PR and brand considerations, at the discretion of the team. However, there is a minimum reward of __USD 50 000__ and a maximum reward of __USD 500 000__.

Maple requires KYC to be done for all bug bounty hunters submitting a report and wanting a reward. The information needed is a government-issued photo ID as well as a proof of address issued within the last 3 months (eg. utility bill). 

All known issues highlighted in the following audit reports are considered to be out-of-scope:
- [https://github.com/maple-labs/maple-v2-audits/blob/main/README.md](https://github.com/maple-labs/maple-v2-audits/blob/main/README.md) 

In addition, all issues related to 4626 compliance are considered out of scope of this program. 

Payouts are handled by the __Maple__ team directly and are denominated in USD. However, payouts are done in __USDC__ or __SYRUP__, at the discretion of the team.

## Program overview

Founded in 2019 and led by a team of former bankers and credit investment professionals aiming to improve upon legacy capital markets, Maple is an institutional capital network that provides the infrastructure for credit experts to run on-chain lending businesses and connects institutional lenders and borrowers. Built with both traditional financial institutions and decentralized finance leaders, Maple is transforming capital markets by combining industry-standard compliance and due diligence with the transparent and frictionless lending enabled by smart contracts and blockchain technology. Maple is the gateway to growth for financial institutions, pool delegates and companies seeking capital on-chain.

For more information about Maple, please visit [https://maple.finance/](https://maple.finance/).
